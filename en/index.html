FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is palmhillsdev.com the official Palm Hills website?

No. palmhillsdev.com is a marketing platform owned by Ibrahim El Garhy, Sales Deputy Manager at Palm Hills. The company's official website is palmhillsdevelopments.com, and contracts are signed directly with the company.

What is the lowest down payment?

Hacienda Ras El Hekma: 5%; Badya Palm Hills: 1.5%; 97 Hills: 2.8%.

What is the longest installment period?

Hacienda Ras El Hekma: 10 years; Badya Palm Hills: 12 years; 97 Hills: 10 years.

When is delivery?

Hacienda Ras El Hekma: 2030; Badya Palm Hills: 2028 – 2031; 97 Hills: 2030, according to the developer.

Are the prices shown final?

Prices are indicative and may be changed by the developer without notice. The company is the final reference at the time of contracting.
