About the project

About Hacienda Ras El Hekma

Everything you need to know in a minute

Hacienda Ras El Hekma is Palm Hills Developments' newest North Coast destination, covering around 1,400 feddans in Ras El Hekma with a 4.8 km sandy beachfront.

The master plan is by OBMI and is laid out around lagoons and green spaces, with beach homes, chalets, twin houses and villas set in rows stepping back from the sea. All units are delivered fully finished with air conditioning and kitchens, with delivery expected in 2030.
