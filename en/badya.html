About the project

About Badya Palm Hills

Everything you need to know in a minute

Badya is an integrated city by Palm Hills Developments in 6th of October on Al Wahat Road, divided into 6 districts, each with its own plan and services, connected through a central hub called The Core.

The city is built around a smart, green concept, with Badya International University, schools, sports clubs and walking and cycling trails. Units available now are in Districts 2 and 6: fully finished one- to three-bedroom apartments, plus core & shell town houses and villas.
