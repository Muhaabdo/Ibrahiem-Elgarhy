About the project

About Bamboo III

Everything you need to know in a minute

Bamboo III is the third phase of Bamboo by Palm Hills Developments, inside Palm Hills October near Gezira Club and overlooking the 26th of July Axis.

The project covers around 56 feddans with lagoons, water features and wide green areas. Homes are family homes with a garden or penthouse plus middle and corner town houses — all fully finished.

FAQ

Bamboo III: frequently asked questions

Where is Bamboo III located?

Bamboo III is on the 26th of July Axis in 6th of October, close to West Cairo's largest malls, universities and clubs. It is developed by Palm Hills Developments.

What are the starting prices in Bamboo III?

Prices start from EGP 27,457,000. Available units: Family Home with Garden from EGP 27,457,000; Family Home with Penthouse from EGP 28,750,000; Town House Middle from EGP 34,342,000; Town House Corner from EGP 35,303,000.

What payment plans are available in Bamboo III?

5% down payment with installments over up to 10 years.

What unit types and sizes are available in Bamboo III?

Family Home with Garden (182 m²); Family Home with Penthouse (191 – 192 m²); Town House Middle (Built-up area 221 m², land area 249 – 254 m²); Town House Corner (Built-up area 222 m², land area 249 – 254 m²).

When is Bamboo III delivered?

Delivery is in 2029, according to the developer.

Are Bamboo III homes finished?

Yes, all the units shown are delivered fully finished.

No. The developer is Palm Hills Developments. VIBE Real Estate is an independent broker that presents the project and helps you choose a unit; contracts are signed directly with the developer.
